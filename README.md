### 🩺 Open-source & OSAID

I'm a community member of the [Open-Source Automated Insulin Delivery](https://openaps.org/) movement. I contribute to closed-loop insulin delivery apps used by people with Type 1 diabetes around the world — software where reliability genuinely matters.

- [**Trio**](https://github.com/nightscout/Trio) — an automated insulin delivery app for iOS based on the APS algorithm
- [**Loop**](https://github.com/LoopKit/Loop) — the original open-source looping app for iOS
- [**DanaKit**](https://github.com/bastiaanv/DanaKit) - The official pump driver for the Dana pumps
- [**MedtrumKit**](https://github.com/jbr7rr/MedtrumKit) - The official pump driver for the Medtrum patch pump
- [**EversenseKit**](https://github.com/bastiaanv/Eversense) - The official CGM driver for the Eversense E3/365
- [**AccuChekKit**](https://github.com/bastiaanv/AccuChek) - The official CGM driver for the AccuChek CGM
